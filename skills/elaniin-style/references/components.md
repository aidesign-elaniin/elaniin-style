## 1. Nav and mega-menu

**Nav bar** (`nav-bar`, `nav-item`, `nav-toggle`; spec in `websites.md`): 64px, sticky, glass (surface at 80% under 8px blur), adopts the color of the section under it (LIGHT white 80% + `border-default`; BLUE Excellence Blue 80% + `border-on-dark`; BLACK Elaniin Black 80%); 150ms, colors only. Only whole SECTIONS declare `data-nav-theme="blue|black"` when their own background is dark; the color of the SECTION wins, never the resources inside it (a white cases section keeps the bar light while it crosses dark case stages; the stages must not declare a theme). While a menu panel or the mobile sheet is open the bar stays light. Logo: the logotipo, a link to `#home` of the current language. FOUR entries in this order on the Elaniin site: Solutions (menu), Engagement models (menu), Resources (menu), About (a plain link); then the language switch, a Blog `secondary md` and the CTA (`primary md` or `dark md`, picked once for the whole site; "Start a project", the hero says "Let's talk" so they never repeat). No search, no sign-in. Over the dark themes the Blog button is transparent with a `border-on-dark-strong` outline and white text. Mobile (<1024px): a 40px hamburger (Menu/X, 1px border-strong, radius 6) opens a full-height white SHEET under the bar (top 64px; utility `sheet`; not a side drawer): three accordions (Solutions open by default, Engagement models, Resources), the plain About link (h-14, display h4), and at the bottom the language switch, the primary lg and the Blog secondary lg, full width; scroll locked; closes on Escape, link click or growing to desktop. The primary CTA stays visible in the header at every width; the Blog button is hidden below 1024px.

**Mega-menu** (`layout/mega-menu.tsx`): desktop dropdown panels, one shared panel flush under the bar (white, `border-default`, radius 8, `shadow-menu`, container width, columns split by 1px lines, column padding 32). Heading: body-sm 500 muted, normal case (a group label, not an eyebrow). Trigger 40px, padding-x 12, body-sm 500, radius 6, `surface-subtle` BACKGROUND when open or on hover (the text color does not change; white at 10% on the dark themes), chevron 16px rotates 180°. Behavior: opens on hover (80ms intent), click/tap or ArrowDown (focus enters the panel); switching triggers is instant, the panel height animates to its content (300ms emphasized) and the content cross-slides 24px in the direction of travel in 200ms emphasized (`menu-swap`); closes 140ms after the pointer leaves, on Escape (focus returns to the trigger), click outside, or focus leaving the header; Tab from an open trigger enters the panel. Three kinds:
- **solutions**: THREE cards inset 8px (gap 8, radius card), one per solution, each with its look (`solution-software|ai|cloud`), icon-box, title (h3 size), description, 4 sublinks on a hairline; footer strip on `surface-subtle` (a question + a tertiary button).
- **engagement**: THREE inset DARK cards (`card-glow-quiet`, Elaniin Black, `border-on-dark`, `border-on-dark-strong` on hover, radius card, padding 32), icon-box dark, name display h3 white, one body-sm line on-dark-secondary and, bottom right, the model's isometric scene (240px wide, running only while the panel is open); the whole card is one link, an arrow at the top right slides in on hover; same help strip under them.
- **resources**: two columns (2fr rows + 3fr featured work). Left: three rich rows (icon-box + title body-lg 500 + description body-sm secondary; hover subtle, radius 8). Right (from 1024px): the **featured work** (`visual-column`): a heading ("Featured cases", display h4 500 with an arrow in link color that slides 4px; "Results that are measured" body-sm secondary below) and TWO VERTICAL CARDS side by side (8px apart): Excellence Blue, `border-on-dark`, radius card, overflow hidden, the whole-card `card-glow`. They share four rows through subgrid (a 40px logo slot, the title row, the button row, the interface row) so titles, buttons and cropped interfaces align in both languages. Top: 24px padding, the client logo WHITE (the tight file without safe area, `brightness-0 invert`), the case title display h4 on-dark, a white 40px button ("Read the case", expand icon). Bottom: the REAL scene of the case, scaled down (70-82%, about 360px wide), cut by the right edge and the bottom of a 190px crop, 24px from the left. Content mounts only while the panel is open; rests on its finished frame under reduced motion.

## 3. Logo strip

Hairline grid: the label cell plus FOUR logo cells (`sections/logo-strip.tsx`); 5 columns from 1024px, label full width + four in one row from 768px, 2x2 below. Label in display h4 (max 20ch); logos 48px high in 144px cells, width limit 80% of the cell, contained; grayscale at 80% opacity, full color and opacity on hover (150ms, color only); cell hover `surface-subtle`. Each cell crossfades through its region's logos (3s per logo, 0.3s fades), cell j one logo behind cell j-1, cells change 400ms apart left to right, so two cells never show the same logo; with four logos or fewer nothing rotates; only opacity animates; under reduced motion each cell rests on its first logo. Cells `aria-hidden`; the names are announced once in a visually hidden list. **Regions**: the strip shows the logos of the visitor's region (`world` / `sv`, `templates/i18n/region.ts`); decided by the proxy WITHOUT asking for location: `?country=` override (also sets the cookie) → the platform country header (`x-vercel-ip-country`) → the cookie `region` (one year, SameSite Lax); default `world`. Pages stay static; the strip is a client leaf that reads the cookie and fades in once it knows. A real client logo and name need the client's authorization before going public.
